Input

Keep the useful part.

Paste a full Showdown export, a Pokémon Champions rental-style list, or just six names separated by slashes. Moves let the Lab judge offensive coverage; typing alone still gives a defensive read.

The Lab recognizes common Regulation M-B Pokémon and moves. If a Pokémon is unfamiliar, choose its typing manually after analysis.

What it can answer

“Can I hit every relevant type?” “Do three Pokémon fold to Ground?” “Am I relying on one Water resist?” It gives a type-framework check before you move to damage calcs and board-position testing.

What it will not pretend to answer
  • Typing is not a full doubles matchup.
  • Abilities, items, speed control, and moves with conditional types need your judgment.
  • A green grid does not mean a team has a winning game plan.
Mega note

Charizardite X is read as Fire/Dragon. Other recognized Mega items use their known defensive typing; the preview roster still matters in actual games.

Method note. Offensive coverage records whether at least one recognized attacking move is super-effective against each single defensive type. Defensive coverage counts the team’s weaknesses, resistances, and immunities to each attacking type. This is a screening tool for type structure—not a damage calculator, legality checker, or complete doubles matchup verdict.
