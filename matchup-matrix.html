DON’T PRETEND

Mark uncertainty honestly.

“Unplanned” is useful information. It tells you what to test before a tournament rather than creating false certainty from a generic matchup score.

NAME THE RESOURCE

Preserve a specific answer.

Write a Pokémon, move, speed-control option, or weather reset—not simply “health.” The value is in knowing what cannot be traded early.

MAKE TURN 4 REAL

Describe a board state.

“Remove Pelipper; keep Grass attacker alive” is better than “be ahead.” A tangible condition makes your early turns easier to evaluate.
