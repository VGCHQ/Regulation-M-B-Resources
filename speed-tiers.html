What the numbers say about Regulation M-B

Format speed insights

These takeaways are derived from the complete legal speed table and the current tournament Choice Scarf dataset. They are memory aids, not claims about Pokémon viability.

223

Clears the entire natural roster

The unboosted MAX+ ceiling is 222. Reaching 223 guarantees moving before every unboosted legal Pokémon in the same priority bracket.

170

Clears the Garchomp / Floette line

Garchomp and Mega Floette cap at 169. A final 170 also clears the central base-100 line at 167.

185 / 190

Two clean fast-offense targets

185 clears max+ Whimsicott at 184; 190 clears max+ Sneasler and Mega Froslass at 189.

178

The crowded base-110 tie cluster

Mega Staraptor, Mega Metagross, Mega Raichu X, Gengar, Froslass, Raichu and several others share this MAX+ value. Species alone does not break that tie.

254

Doubled mid-speed can beat Scarf Garchomp

A MAX+ base-64 Pokémon reaches 127 and doubles to 254—one point above max+ Choice Scarf Garchomp at 253.

244 → 268

Mega Swampert's nature changes the race

Neutral max-invested Mega Swampert reaches 122 and 244 in rain, while MAX+ reaches 134 and 268. The first loses to max+ Scarf Hydreigon and Garchomp; the second clears both.

111

A -2 drop flips the natural ceiling

Halving 222 produces 111. That puts even the fastest natural Pokémon below max+ Kingambit and Mega Mawile at 112.

Usage ≠ speed

Read item frequency and speed rank separately

Basculegion leads recorded Scarf usage, but faster top-20 Scarf ceilings include Meowscarada at 288, Alolan Ninetales at 265, Garchomp at 253, and the base-100 line at 250.

Data and verification.

Legal roster and Speed values: Pokémon Zone's Regulation M-B Speed Tiers, which lists 315 legal Pokémon/forms and calculates Level 50 values with maximum IVs and 32 Speed SP for “Max.” The page was checked on 28 July 2026.

Choice Scarf usage: Pokémon Zone's Choice Scarf item page, sourced from completed Limitless tournaments. At the check date it covered 265 tournaments, 60,736 battles and 7,001 Choice Scarf uses. The radar reproduces the page's listed top 20; the full table separately calculates every legal holder's hypothetical ceiling.

Regulation eligibility: the official Regulation Set M-B notice, active from 17 June through 2 September 2026.

Visual assets: animated sprites are served by Pokémon Showdown, with static sprite fallbacks when an animation is unavailable.
